# Welcome to Verifact

Verifact is an AI-powered content verification platform that helps you detect misinformation, verify claims, and ensure content integrity at scale.

## What is Verifact?

Verifact uses a multi-paradigm verification approach combining:

- **LLM reasoning** - Advanced language models analyze claims contextually
- **Search verification** - Cross-referencing against authoritative sources
- **Logical consistency** - Detecting internal contradictions
- **Source reputation** - Evaluating the credibility of cited sources
- **Temporal analysis** - Checking if information is outdated
- **Synthetic detection** - Identifying AI-generated content
- **Contextual analysis** - Understanding nuance and framing

## Key Features

### Multi-Paradigm Verification

Each piece of content is analyzed through multiple verification methods simultaneously, providing a comprehensive assessment with confidence scores.

### Automatic Escalation

Low-confidence verifications are automatically flagged for human review, ensuring accuracy on edge cases.

### Real-time Webhooks

Get instant notifications when verifications complete or require attention.

### Team Collaboration

Invite team members, assign roles, and manage permissions for your organization.
