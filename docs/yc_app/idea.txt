Our Solution: Multi-Method Verification Platform
The Core Idea
Like Stripe for payments - offer multiple methods and let users choose.
Stripe doesn't force one payment type: credit cards for most purchases, bank transfers for large B2B, digital wallets for mobile, crypto for web3.
We don't force one verification method:
Semantic for speed (blog posts, low-stakes)
Reasoning for general accuracy (customer support)
BiPRM for complex reasoning (medical, legal)
Ensemble for maximum accuracy (critical decisions)
Formal for mathematical certainty (crypto, code)
How Customers Use It
Simple API:
Send us: The claim to verify, which method to use (or combination), optional context
Get back: Verdict (TRUE / FALSE / UNCERTAIN), confidence score, explanation of the decision
Customer decides: Accept the output or flag for human review
The Innovation: Mix and Match with Custom Weights
Here's what makes us unique - users can combine multiple verifiers with their own custom weighting:
Example 1: Medical AI Company
"I want my verification to be: 60% BiPRM (catches reasoning errors), 30% Tool-Augmented (verifies facts against medical databases), 10% Formal (for drug dosage calculations)"
Result: Custom verification optimized for medical use case
Example 2: Legal AI Company
"I want: 50% Ensemble (maximum general accuracy), 50% Tool-Augmented (searches case law to verify citations)"
Result: Catches both reasoning errors AND fake citations
Example 3: Crypto Startup
"I want: 100% Formal Verification (mathematical proof)"
Result: 100% certainty for smart contract verification
