Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stripe for LLM verification. 7 methods, one API.

What is your company going to make?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Check.ai is the multi-method verification platform for AI outputs. Like Stripe
lets you choose credit cards, ACH, or crypto for payments, we let you choose
semantic, reasoning, tool-augmented, ensemble, or formal verification for
AI accuracy--or combine them with custom weights.

Problem: Every company deploying AI faces the same nightmare. Air Canada's
chatbot invented a refund policy (Feb 2024)--customer sued, won, set legal
precedent. Medical AI invents symptoms--malpractice. Legal AI cites fake
cases--sanctions. Average cost per incident: $2.4M.

Current solutions force one approach: Guardrails AI (rules only, can't verify
facts), Cleanlab (single score, can't customize), Harmonic (math proofs only,
$1.45B valuation but 99% of use cases aren't math).

Our solution: 7 verification methods in one platform:
- Semantic: 100ms, low-stakes content
- Entropy: 200ms, consistency checks
- Reasoning: 2-3s, 93% accuracy for general use
- BiPRM: 3-5s, bidirectional reasoning (31.9% better than baseline)
- Tool-augmented: 3-5s, web search + fact databases
- Ensemble: 5-10s, multi-model voting (95.6% accuracy)
- Formal: Variable, mathematical proofs

Custom weighting example: "60% BiPRM + 30% Tool + 10% Formal" for medical AI.
No competitor offers this flexibility.

Why did you pick this idea? Do you have domain expertise?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Justin (Mastercard compliance, 400+ application audits): Saw enterprise
paranoia about AI accuracy firsthand. Organizations can't deploy AI at scale
without verification--it's a compliance blocker.

Michael (autonomous AI agents, DOD-funded sensor systems): Saw the cascading
failure problem. One hallucination in an agent workflow breaks code, breaks
subsequent decisions. Built AI systems where accuracy was non-negotiable.

Why this solution: Verification systems today are single-path. When that one
path fails (misses a negation, hallucinates a citation), nothing catches it.
But run 5 independent paths that fail on different inputs, and when they
agree, accuracy compounds. Our 7-method weighted consensus is the first
platform to offer this.

Why now:
- Legal precedent: Air Canada (Feb 2024) established companies are liable
- FDA (Jan 2025): Medical devices must have documented verification
- CA AB 3030 (Jan 2026): Healthcare providers must disclose AI-generated content
- 78% of enterprises using AI in production (up from 55% last year)
- Inference cost down 95% since 2020--verification is now economically viable

Who are your competitors?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Direct competitors:
- Guardrails AI ($7.5M seed): Rule-based only. Can't verify facts.
- Cleanlab ($25M Series A): Single trustworthiness score. Can't customize.
- Harmonic ($295M, $1.45B valuation): Math proofs only. 99% of use cases
  aren't math.
- AIMon: Single detection model. One method.

What they ALL miss: Single-path verification is fundamentally brittle.

Our moat:
1. Only multi-method platform: 7 methods + custom weighting across ALL domains
2. Data lock-in: We collect verification stats and historical data. Dashboard
   shows trends, accuracy over time, cost analysis. Hard to leave your history.
3. Easy integration: Simple SDKs (TypeScript, Python, LangChain, LlamaIndex).
   Once integrated, switching cost is high.
4. Network effects: Each new method attracts new customer segment, funds next
   method
