What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

Check is an API that evaluates confidence and accuracy in AI-generated text. Like Stripe offers credit cards, ACH, and crypto for payments, we offer 7 verification methods: semantic similarity, entropy checking, LLM-as-judge reasoning, bidirectional premise reasoning, tool-augmented web search, multi-model ensemble voting, and formal mathematical proofs. With more on the way!

Customers pick which methods to use based on their needs. A legal AI company might use 50% ensemble + 50% tool-augmented to catch both reasoning errors and fake citations. A medical AI might use 60% BiPRM + 30% tool + 10% formal. A blog writer uses semantic only for speed.

As AI grows in speed and accessibility, customers prioritize trust and accuracy.

One API call. Choose your methods. Get a verdict (TRUE/FALSE/UNCERTAIN) with confidence score and explanation.

We realized this major opportunity through our combined experience in AI and tech. AI development and usage is rapidly increasing, and users are growing more and more reliant on trust and accuracy.

Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?

Guardrails AI ($7.5M seed): Rule-based safety only. Doesn’t verify factual accuracy of AI-generated statements.
Cleanlab ($30M combined seed and series A funding): Single trustworthiness score. Doesn’t incorporate multiple methodologies to customize trustworthiness score.
Harmonic ($295M, $1.45B valuation): Math proofs only. 99% of use cases aren't math.

What they all miss: Single-method verification is fundamentally brittle. When that one method fails (misses a negation, hallucinates a citation), nothing catches it. Different domains need different verification approaches. A medical AI needs different checks than a legal AI than a coding assistant.

We're the only platform offering multiple methods with custom weighting. Customers can combine methods to match their specific accuracy requirements.
